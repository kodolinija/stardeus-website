<p>Guests are survivors rescued from derelict ships and stations. They keep their own faction, and they eat, sleep and socialise alongside your crew - but they never take a work order.</p>

<p>Living guests are listed in their own Guests group in the beings list at the top of the screen - separate from your crew.</p>

<p>To send a guest home, hail a ship or space station belonging to their faction and choose <span class="accent"><span class="accent"><b>Send Beings</b></span></span>. Their people will come collect everyone of that faction currently aboard your ship, pay you for each person returned, and their attitude towards you will improve.</p>

<p>This requires a working <span class="nobr"><a href="/codex/database/objects/devices/trade-portal/">Trade Portal</a></span>, and only picks up guests who are not tied to a delivery quest.</p>

<p>If a guest's own faction raids your ship, they will defect and fight alongside the attackers.</p>

<p>Guests left behind on a derelict when you hyperjump away are gone for good.</p>
